Welcome to FractalLauncher

Let's take a quick tour — it only takes a minute.

Step 1: Add an account

You need an account to play. Open the Accounts page and click "+ Add Account" — you can use a Microsoft account or play offline.

Step 2: Create a profile

A profile remembers a Minecraft version, mod loader and RAM. Open the Profiles page and click "+ New Profile".

Step 3: Home & Play

Home is where you launch. Pick your profile, choose an account, then hit Play.

Explore the launcher

You're all set!
